Plain words

Range
The high and low of one hour's candle. Two prices. Everything is measured against them.
Sweep
Price pushes past an edge far enough to trip the stop orders resting there, then turns back.
Close-back
That same candle finishes inside the range again. Without it the break was real and there is no setup. This condition never bends.
H4 bias
Which way the four-hour trend points. On your own data, setups with it returned +0.40R; against it, −0.17R. Same model, opposite result.
R
One R is what you lose if the stop hits. Twice your risk is +2R. Counting in R means numbers mean the same at any account size.
Expectancy
Average R across every trade. Positive means it makes money over time. The only number that settles anything.
Profit factor
Gross wins divided by gross losses. Below 1.00 means it lost money. There is no reading that rescues that.
Drawdown
The deepest fall from a peak. Tells you what you'd have had to sit through.
Why stages
None of this is proven yet. The stages exist so live money arrives after the evidence, not before it.
