System

Services and chain
Exness quotes reach MetaTrader 5 on the Windows box. A read-only expert advisor posts each closed 15-minute bar to this server, which stores it with the spread that was live at the time. Cron scans the file every fifteen minutes, logs anything the models find, and scores what has resolved.

Nothing in this chain places an order.
Nothing here is deleted. A falsified model with its numbers attached is worth more than a forgotten one — it stops the same idea coming back wearing a different name.
